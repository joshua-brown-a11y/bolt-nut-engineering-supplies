QUALITY ENGINEERING & FASTENING SOLUTIONS

BOLT NUT & ENGINEERING SUPPLIES (PTY) LTD (BNES) since its inception in 1988 with 100% B.E.E shareholding, has grown to be a leader in the fastener & engineering industries.

With strong representation in South Africa and a full complement of staff hailing from historically disadvantaged backgrounds, we have devised a culture of outstanding service reliability and competitive pricing.

Drawing on the industry experience and knowledge of our staff, our emphasis on approach is built on client development and attaining optimal service delivery on all levels with maximum participation from our clients.
